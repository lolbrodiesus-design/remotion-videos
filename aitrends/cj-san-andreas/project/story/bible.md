# Your Life as CJ — story bible

## Logline and tone
A street kid comes home to bury his mother and ends up owning the city. Tone: wry, cinematic,
slightly tragic — the narrator sounds like a friend telling you how it really went down.
Second person ("you"), so every hero shot frames the viewer as CJ.

## Locations
1. **street** — Grove-style residential street. Mood `dusk`. Key light: low sun down the street,
   amber, with long palm shadows. Houses with porch lights, chain-link fence, power lines, palms.
   Layers: sky gradient → skyline of palms/roofs → houses → road → foreground fence.
2. **home** — CJ's porch and front room. Mood `interiorWarm` outside / `dusk` inside. Key: a single
   warm bulb. Props: faded green graffiti, boarded window, framed family photo, sagging couch.
3. **casino** — Mood `interiorWarm`. Key: chandelier + slot machine glow. Props: green felt table,
   slot machines, chip stacks, a duffel bag.
4. **airfield** — Mood `golden`. Key: big low sun on the horizon. Props: hangar, windsock,
   cracked runway with centre line, a parked prop plane, fence posts.
5. **cemetery** — Mood `dusk`. Key: sun behind the headstones, long shadows. Props: headstones,
   a mound of flowers, a lone tree, a low iron fence.
6. **rooftop** — Mood `night` graded to dusk. Key: the city's own lights below. Props: parapet,
   money stacks, antenna, distant skyline windows.
7. **city** — Mood `night`. Skyline of lit windows for establishing wides.
8. **void** — Mood `void`. Blueprint grid, for the money and territory diagrams.

## Motifs
- **The green cap** — CJ's only colour, present in every single shot he's in, including the suit.
- **Bullet-time** — one signature freeze (shot s008), droplets hanging, cool tint, `100x` badge.
- **Counters** — every number the narrator says appears as an animated counter (0 → businesses → territories → airfield → fortune).
- **Green zones** on a blueprint map, spreading as territory is taken.
- **Sun behind the headstones** — the funeral is the only shot where the sun is in front of CJ.
